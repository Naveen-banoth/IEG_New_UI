HIPAA Compliance Verified

Scimax maintains HIPAA compliance in the United States. HIPAA compliance involves fulfilling the requirements of the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act of 1996, its subsequent amendments, and related legislation such as the Health Information Technology for Economic and Clinical Health (HITECH) Act.

Protected health information (PHI) handled in Scimax is safeguarded under the HIPAA Privacy Rule. Access is limited to authorized users for permitted treatment, payment, and healthcare operations, with role-based controls that keep investigator, sponsor, and operations teams within their approved scope.

The HIPAA Security Rule guides how Scimax protects electronic PHI. Platform controls include encrypted transport (TLS), encrypted storage for sensitive records, unique user authentication, session timeouts, and audit logging of access and key administrative actions.

Under the Breach Notification Rule and HITECH Act expectations, Scimax maintains documented incident response practices. Suspected unauthorized access or disclosure is investigated promptly, contained, and communicated according to applicable federal and organizational requirements.

Administrative safeguards include workforce training, access authorization procedures, contingency planning, and vendor oversight for systems that touch PHI. Physical and technical safeguards cover facility access, workstation security, and continuous monitoring of identity and access events.

Business associate obligations are addressed through contractual and operational controls with partners that create, receive, maintain, or transmit PHI on behalf of covered entities using Scimax. Data retention and disposal practices are aligned with program, legal, and compliance policies.

Users should only access applications and documents needed for their assigned responsibilities, avoid sharing credentials, and report suspected security issues immediately. Scimax support can assist with access requests, MFA reset workflows, and compliance documentation for audits.

This overview is provided for transparency on the login experience and does not replace your organization's policies, BAAs, or formal compliance attestations. For detailed security whitepapers or audit packages, contact your Scimax administrator or customer success representative.
